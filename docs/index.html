How it works

From resume to reply, with you in control.

Kold does the searching, writing and follow-up tracking every day, so you don't have to. You make every decision that matters.

  • Upload your resumeYour profile builds itself. Every detail is checked against your resume, so nothing is invented. Edit anything.
  • Swipe through genuine matchesListings come from employers' official careers boards and trusted public job boards: no scraping, scam warnings included, and each card says why it matched.
  • Find the right personKold only uses email addresses the company publishes itself, shows exactly where each one was found, and never guesses.
  • Approve every emailA tailored resume and a personal email are drafted into Today. Edit, then send now or at the recipient's morning in their time zone.
  • Follow up politelyAt most two follow-ups, threaded under your first email and drafted for your review. Any reply stops them instantly.
  • Every reply, in one placeReplies are labelled: interested, a question, not now, declined, out of office, bounced. Answer yourself, or let Kold draft a reply you edit.
The app

Five screens. Zero inbox digging.

🏠

Home

Your first stop: replies that need you, emails to approve, deadlines this week, new matches and your pipeline at a glance.

❄️

Discover

A swipeable deck of verified openings with a match "temperature", the reasons behind it, the source and a trust score.

✍️

Today

Your daily review queue. Pre-send checks flag placeholders, spammy phrasing and any claim your resume doesn't back.

💬

Inbox

One conversation per company with the full back-and-forth, including replies sent as a new email or by a colleague.

🗂️

Tracker

Saved → Applied → Emailing → Replied → Interview → Offer, with notes, deadlines and reminders.

🔔

Activity log

A timestamped record of everything Kold did for you. Nothing happens off the record.

Principles

Genuine. Transparent. Yours.

✓ You approve everything

No email leaves without your tap

Daily cap of 25 emails, two follow-ups at most, and an automatic pause if someone is unhappy or out of office.

✓ Private by design

Your own AI, on your machine

Kold runs an open-weight model locally, with no third-party AI services. It reads only your outreach conversations, never the rest of your inbox.

✓ No invented facts

Grounded in your resume

Every number and claim in a draft must come from your resume. Anything else is flagged before you send.

✓ Real listings only

Official sources, live postings

Listings the employer takes down disappear, and remote roles are shown only if they accept candidates in India.

Run it

Open the link. Or run the full version.

Kold Lite runs entirely in your browser: open the app, choose "Add to Home Screen", add your resume and connect Gmail. Your data stays on your device, the job feed is refreshed from official employer boards every morning, and matching runs every time you open it. Nothing to install, nothing to pay.

Kold Full adds a server that works while your phone is off: replies checked around the clock, a bigger local AI model, and the same data on all your devices. It is free too:

# 1. Local AI model
ollama pull qwen3.5:4b

# 2. Server
git clone https://github.com/JiviteshKumar/kold.git
cd kold/api
python -m venv .venv
.venv/Scripts/pip install -r requirements.txt     # macOS/Linux: .venv/bin/pip
cp .env.example .env                              # add KOLD_GMAIL_SANDBOX=1 to try it without real email
.venv/Scripts/python -m uvicorn kold.main:app --port 8000

# 3. App (new terminal)
cd kold/web
npm install
npm run dev -- -p 3100                            # open http://localhost:3100

To send from your own Gmail, follow "Gmail setup" in the README. On a phone, open the app over HTTPS and choose "Add to Home Screen".
